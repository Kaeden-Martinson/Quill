        How the settings work together

        For each word, the model gives every possible next word a chance. Ollama then narrows that list in order: top_k keeps the likeliest few; temperature reshapes the chances (low favours the top word, high evens them out, 0 always takes the top word); top_p keeps the likeliest words until their chances add up to the cutoff; min_p drops any word less likely than that share of the top word. One word is then drawn from what's left.

        Click any word in a result to see its candidates. The chances shown are among the 10 likeliest candidates, which almost always cover nearly all of it.
